# Multibranded
Multibranded’s Mission Statement
Multibranded is a diceless collaborative storytelling game with an accompanying loose ruleset. Though Multibranded may be played as a classic TTRPG, players and DMs alike may find the experience more enjoyable when using the rules as a suggestion, rather than law. 
The goal of Multibranded is to tell a unique and interesting story, with your characters at its center. The systems written here are designed for the purpose of facilitating and nurturing the creativity of players. If at any time the rules would fly in the face of this guiding principle, then they should be discarded or fudged in favor of a greater story.

There is currently a Google Doc with the majority of the ruleset on it. It's kind of rough at the moment. 
Most of the information pertaining to Brands can be found within the files in this repository.
This repository will eventually be updated with a full wiki for the game, including everything that you will need for players and DMs to enjoy the full experience.

# Character Sheet Applet and the Wiki
If you download the repository, you can run the character sheet app (CharacterSheetLayout.html) and the brand wiki app (BrandWiki.html) in your browser of choice.
This has been tested on Firefox, Safari (yes it works on mobile but not super well), VSCode Preview & Live Server
I have not yet had issues running either applet on either of my devices. This does not guarantee they will run on all devices, but they ARE programmed with JS HTML, so I would HOPE they would.
